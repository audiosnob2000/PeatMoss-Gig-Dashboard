1. Who we are

Gig Dashboard is run by [OPERATOR NAME AND ADDRESS] (“we”, “us”). This page explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and what choices you have. Questions: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

2. What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address and your password. Your password is handled by Google’s sign-in service and we cannot see it. The name shown to your bandmates is taken from your email address.
  • Your bands. Each band’s name and time zone, which bands you belong to, your role in each (owner, admin or member), and when you joined.
  • Band content. Whatever you and your bandmates add to a band: shows and their details, setlists, charts and chart links, notes, availability, checklists, the stage plot and similar items.
  • Your personal items. Your mileage log (date, distance and a label such as a venue), your private chart library, and your personal settings. Some settings, such as a home address you enter for mileage, are kept on your device.
  • Notifications. If you turn on notifications, a token that identifies your phone or browser so we can send gig reminders and new-setlist alerts.
  • Google Calendar, if you connect it. The app asks Google for permission to read and write events on your calendar so shows can be synced. We keep a Google token on our server so syncing can work. You can disconnect in Settings, and you can also remove the app’s access any time in your Google account.
  • Your location, if you allow it. If you use mileage tracking or “use my location”, the app uses your device’s location to measure distance or to find an address. The saved trip contains the date, distance and a label, not a continuous record of where you have been.
  • Billing information. Your Stripe customer identifier, your plan, your subscription status and renewal dates. We also keep a card fingerprint, an anonymous code Stripe creates for a card, so that each card can have only one free trial. We never see or store your card number: it goes directly to Stripe.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show you your bands’ content, and keep it in sync between your bandmates.
  • To send reminders and alerts you have turned on.
  • To take payment, manage your subscription, and send you a confirmation and other billing messages by email.
  • To keep the service safe and to enforce one free trial per person and per card.

We do not sell your information. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

4. Who can see it

  • Your bandmates can see band content and the band’s member list (names and email addresses) while they have access. They cannot see your mileage log or private chart library.
  • Companies that help us run the app. They handle information only to provide their service to us:
    • Google (Firebase): sign-in, our database, our server functions, push notifications, and Google Calendar if you connect it.
    • Stripe: payments and subscriptions.
    • Resend: sending our emails to you.
    • GitHub Pages: hosting the app’s pages.
  • Places and weather lookups. To show weather and find addresses, the app sends place names or coordinates to OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) and Open-Meteo, which do not receive your account details. If you tap a map or Google Drive link, that service opens and receives the request as it would from any link.
  • If the law requires it, we may have to share information with authorities.

5. What is stored on your device

The app saves your sign-in and some preferences, such as your settings and an in-progress mileage trip, in your browser’s local storage so it works smoothly and offline. We do not use advertising cookies.

6. How long we keep it

  • Band content and your personal items stay while your account and the band exist. When a show, set or chart is deleted from a band, it sits in “Recently deleted” for 30 days and is then erased.
  • If your subscription lapses, your information is kept so that everything is there when you come back.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and personal data (see section 8). We may keep a few records longer when the law requires, such as payment records.

7. Security

Your data is stored with Google’s cloud services behind access rules so that people can only reach the bands they belong to. Payments are handled by Stripe. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

8. Your choices

  • Access and correction. You can see and edit your information in the app. To ask for a copy of it or a correction, email us.
  • Deletion. To have your account and personal data deleted, email [SUPPORT EMAIL]. Cancel your subscription first so you are not charged again. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Notifications and location. You can turn either off in the app or in your device settings.
  • Google Calendar. Disconnect in Settings, or remove access in your Google account.
  • Marketing email. We do not send marketing email. We send account and billing messages only.

9. Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
